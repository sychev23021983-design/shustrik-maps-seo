Island Terrain STL Models for 3D Printing & CNC

Bring island landscapes into three-dimensional form with digital terrain STL models for 3D printing and CNC relief carving. From rugged coastal inlets to mountain ridges and broad lowlands, these models give island topography a tangible shape for display and creative projects.

Explore the branching coastline of Iceland, the elongated mountain relief of Corsica, the island composition of Japan and the east-to-west landscape of Crete. The collection also includes Bermuda, Cyprus, Dominica and Jamaica, offering a range of island outlines and terrain structures.

Island reliefs can form the centrepiece of a freestanding printed display, an educational terrain exhibit or a wooden interior accent carved on CNC equipment.
