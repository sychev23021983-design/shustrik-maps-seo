Create continent renders, animation scenes and website graphics with the Asia Terrain 3D Model. Use a full-continent view for presentations, or explore the relief with closer camera angles for geography illustrations and digital storytelling.

The model is supplied in C4D, with OBJ/FBX export available on request. It uses Equidistant Conic projection and an open base. The listed texture set includes height, normal, satellite, water-mask, pseudo-colour and boundary-mask maps, giving you options for materials, displacement and shaded-relief visuals.
