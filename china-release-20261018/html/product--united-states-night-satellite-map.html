Description

The United States at night satellite map shows patterns of city lights against the darker surrounding landscape. Bright urban areas become the focus of the composition, offering a distinctive alternative to a daytime satellite view.

Use the image in geographic presentations, editorial layouts and exhibition graphics, or as the focal point of a printed map. The nighttime perspective lends itself to visual storytelling about cities and regions, with the distribution of lights providing the main graphic structure.
