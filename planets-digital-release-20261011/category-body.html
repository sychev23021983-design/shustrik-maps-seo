Planet 3D Models for Renders, Animation and Web Graphics

Build planetary scenes for rendered illustrations, animation sequences, astronomy presentations and website graphics. This collection includes textured globes and alternative Earth visualisations, offering different starting points for views from space and editorial compositions. Explore the wider Solar System collection for lunar models and surface terrain.

Compare the colour-mapped terrain of Mercury, the cloud textures of Venus, the pale atmospheric appearance of Neptune and the ringed Uranus model. For Earth-focused visuals, browse the textured Earth model, the gravity-map model or terrain without water. Check each listing for its included formats, textures and supporting maps.

The examples below show possible uses in educational graphics, animation, website banners and presentation slides. The four featured listings specify C4D models with textures. Colour treatments, lighting and backgrounds illustrate creative scene choices; they do not describe a supplied finished render or animation. Plan any conversion and scene setup around the files included with your chosen model.
