US State 3D Models for Film and Animation

Create US state location graphics, documentary terrain sequences and film chapter transitions with state 3D models. A recognisable state outline helps introduce a setting, while satellite-textured relief can guide a camera shot from an overview towards regional landforms. Combine the terrain with your own typography, material choices and compositing for the style of your film or animated sequence.

The California State Terrain Map 3D Model and Colorado State 3D Terrain Model are listed in C4D with height, normal, satellite, water-mask, pseudo-colour and boundary-mask textures. The product descriptions explain how these maps can support displacement, surface detail and masking. Check the selected model’s geometry and texture resolution before preparing your scene.

The examples show California film-location titles and an animated locator, plus a Colorado mountain-documentary flyover and chapter transition. The state collections below help you find other locations; browse Canadian Province 3D Models or the full 3D Models catalogue for related assets. Included formats and coverage depend on the product.
