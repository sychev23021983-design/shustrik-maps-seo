Country 3D Models for Film and Animation

Use country 3D models to establish a film’s setting, introduce a documentary chapter or connect scenes with an animated geographic transition. A country-wide terrain overview gives viewers context, while closer camera framing can bring mountain ranges and coastlines into focus. Add your own titles, lighting and compositing to develop a visual language suited to the production.

The Italy Terrain 3D Model and China Terrain 3D Model list C4D models, TIF textures and OBJ versions at three levels of detail. Compare their geometry, file sizes and texture requirements when preparing your scene. These are individual product specifications; check the chosen listing for the files it includes.
