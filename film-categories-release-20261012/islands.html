Island 3D Models for Film and Animation

Build island-focused film titles, animated location maps and terrain flyovers with island 3D models. Coastlines, mountain relief and surrounding seabed can establish a setting before a scene begins, or provide a visual link between locations in a documentary. Choose a terrain view that fits your story, then develop camera movement, lighting and titles in your own production workflow.

The Iceland Terrain Without Water 3D Model and Japan 3D Terrain Without Water Model are supplied in C4D with TIF textures. Their product images show both land and surrounding underwater relief. This can support a geographic overview or an oblique terrain shot; review each product’s coverage, geometry and texture specifications before choosing an asset.

The examples below explore an Iceland opening title and flyover, plus a Japan scene transition and documentary locator graphic. Browse the full 3D Models catalogue or compare Country 3D Models for other film locations. Formats and included files vary by product.
