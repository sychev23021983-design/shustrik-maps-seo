Canadian Province 3D Models for Film and Animation

Use Canadian province 3D models for documentary opening sequences, film location graphics and animated terrain flyovers. Provincial outlines offer a clear geographic introduction, while textured relief can add depth to a camera move or chapter transition. Develop the final shot with your own lighting, titles and compositing to connect the map to the wider story.

The British Columbia 3D Terrain Model and Quebec Terrain 3D Map Model are supplied in C4D with height, normal, satellite, water-mask, pseudo-colour and boundary-mask textures. Both listings use Lambert Conformal Conic projection. Review the chosen product’s geometry, coverage and file requirements when planning materials and camera framing.
