3D Models for Rendering, Animation and Web Graphics

Build digital scenes with 3D models of terrain, countries, continents, planets and architectural landmarks. Use them as starting points for rendered illustrations, animation sequences, website banners and presentation graphics. File formats, textures and supporting maps are listed on each product page.

Explore country terrain models and continent models for geographic scenes, or browse the Solar System collection for planetary visualisations. The Europe and Andorra models offer textured terrain in C4D and OBJ, while the Earth model includes satellite imagery, clouds and night maps for space-themed compositions.

The examples below show possible digital applications, from a website hero image to an architectural scene using the Sydney Opera House model. Lighting, backgrounds and graphic treatments illustrate how a model can be presented; they do not describe a supplied finished scene. Check each listing for included files and textures before planning your workflow.
