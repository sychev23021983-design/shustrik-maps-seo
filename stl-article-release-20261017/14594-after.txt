<p>Alternatives to STL serve different purposes. The <a href="https://shustrik-maps.com/understanding-the-obj-file-format-a-simple-explanation/">OBJ format</a> supports static geometry with material references and texture coordinates, although appearance may depend on accompanying MTL and image files. A slicer may import the geometry without retaining those materials or textures.</p>
<p>3MF can package models with defined units and additional resources. Applications may also use it to save a printing project: PrusaSlicer, for example, saves objects, their arrangement and print settings in a 3MF project file. Application-specific settings should not be assumed to transfer unchanged to another slicer.</p>
<p>STL remains useful for exchanging mesh geometry when broader project information is unnecessary. Choose the format according to the information that must survive the transfer and the capabilities of the receiving software.</p>[/woodmart_text_block][/vc_column][/vc_row]
