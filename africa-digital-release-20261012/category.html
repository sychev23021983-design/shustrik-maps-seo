Africa 3D Models for Renders and Animation

Create continent renders, website graphics and animation scenes with the Africa C4D Terrain Model with Texture Maps. Its height, normal, satellite, water-mask, pseudo-colour and boundary-mask maps support different approaches to materials, displacement and shaded-relief visuals. Frame the continental terrain for an overview, or use closer camera angles to explore landforms.

The Africa C4D Terrain Model with TIF Textures offers another terrain variant with a multi-layer TIF texture. Both products are supplied in C4D, use Mercator projection and have an open base; OBJ/FBX export is available on request. Compare the chosen product’s geometry, texture resolution and file requirements before preparing your scene.
