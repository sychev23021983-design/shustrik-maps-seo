Europe 3D Models for Renders and Animation

Build continent overviews, website graphics and animation scenes with the 3D Model of Europe. Its C4D/OBJ files and TIF texture provide a starting point for satellite-style visuals, from a full European map to a closer view of mountainous terrain. This version uses Lambert Conformal Conic projection and a closed base.

The Europe Terrain Map 3D Model offers a different setup: C4D geometry in Equidistant Conic projection, an open base, and height, normal, satellite, water-mask, pseudo-colour and boundary-mask textures. OBJ/FBX export is available on request. Compare the chosen product’s geometry, maps and projection before setting up camera angles, materials or an import into your software.

The examples below explore a website banner, a geography illustration, a documentary title frame and a region-selection interface concept. Browse the Continent 3D Models collection for other regions, or visit the full 3D Models catalogue for related digital assets.
