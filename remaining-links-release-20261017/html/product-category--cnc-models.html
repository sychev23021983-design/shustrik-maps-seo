STL Models for 3D Printing & CNC

Explore digital STL models for 3D printing and CNC carving, with a focus on topographic maps and terrain relief. The collection includes countries, continents, islands and U.S. states, alongside Earth globes, planetary models and decorative designs.

Terrain models bring mountain ranges, plateaus, valleys and coastlines into three-dimensional form. Their relief surfaces provide a starting point for printed geography models, CNC-carved wooden maps and landscape displays for homes, studios and educational spaces.

Browse Countries, Continents, Islands and USA States for regional terrain models, or explore Planets and Art for globes and decorative pieces. This category offers digital model files; the finished objects below illustrate possible applications.
