Earth & Planet STL Models for 3D Printing & CNC

Explore Earth STL models that turn global surfaces into physical display objects. This collection brings together expressive terrain globes, a river-detailed Earth, a modular globe and an artistic geoid interpretation, offering different ways to present our planet in three dimensions.

The exaggerated-relief Earth emphasizes elevation differences, while the Earth model with rivers combines terrain with river detail. The 12-part modular Earth globe provides a segmented construction, and the geoid height model explores an artistic, deliberately exaggerated gravity-inspired surface.

These digital files provide starting points for printed educational globes, assembled display pieces and sculptural projects. The examples below illustrate printing, surface finishing and a wooden CNC-carving concept, with separate stands added for presentation.
