<h2>Height, normal and satellite maps</h2>
<p>Use the 16-bit TIF height map to drive displacement on a surface in your 3D software. The normal map helps show fine relief detail, while the satellite texture provides the color imagery. Map dimensions and bit depths are listed in the specification above.</p>
<h2>Water, elevation color and boundary maps</h2>
<p>The package also includes a water map, a pseudo-color elevation map and a boundary mask. These give you additional inputs for building your scene: the pseudo-color map represents elevation with color, and the boundary mask can be used with your background.</p>
<h2>Working with the terrain model</h2>
<p>The model uses a geographic projection and has an open base. Set your camera, lighting and materials in your 3D software for map illustrations, presentations or animation.
