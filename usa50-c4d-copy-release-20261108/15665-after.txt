<h2>50 separate states for Cinema 4D</h2>
<p>Build USA map scenes with 50 separate state terrain meshes in Cinema 4D. Each state has its own mesh, frontal UV mapping and TIF satellite texture. Show or hide individual states and adjust terrain, base elevation and boundary appearance in your scene.</p>
<h2>Model formats and open bases</h2>
<p>The supplied model format is C4D; OBJ and FBX export is available on request. The meshes have open bases. Before using them for 3D printing or CNC machining, check and prepare the geometry for your machine, material and scale.</p>
<h2>Choosing a USA collection</h2>
<p>Choose this collection when you need separate state terrain objects with satellite textures for rendered maps, presentations or animation. For a collection supplied as STL with closed bases, compare the <a href="https://shustrik-maps.com/product/complete-usa-50-states-topographic-map-3d-model-for-3d-printing-cnc-carving/">50-state STL collection</a>. It is a separate product and still requires preparation for your fabrication workflow.</p>
