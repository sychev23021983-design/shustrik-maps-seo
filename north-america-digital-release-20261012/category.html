North America 3D Models for Renders and Animation

Create continental geography visuals for websites, presentations, educational content and animation scenes. The North America 3D model combines textured terrain with an overview of Greenland, the Arctic islands, the mainland and Central America. Use an overhead view for a clear composition, or an oblique camera and directional lighting to highlight the relief.

For a workflow with multiple texture maps, explore the North America Map 3D Model. Its listing includes height, normal, satellite, water-mask, pseudo-colour and boundary-mask textures. Both products list C4D files and Lambert Conformal Conic projection, with OBJ/FBX conversion offered on request. Check each product for geometry, texture resolution and file requirements before preparing your scene.

The four examples below show a website hero, a relief learning graphic, an animation scene still and a presentation composition. Explore the Continent 3D Models collection for other regions, or browse the full 3D Models catalogue for related digital assets.
