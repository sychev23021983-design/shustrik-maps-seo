<h3>Computer Graphics</h3>
<p>FBX is used to exchange assets for animation, rendering and game development. Applications such as Maya, 3ds Max and MotionBuilder use it to transfer supported model and scene data between stages of production.</p>
<p>For static assets, the transfer may focus on geometry and surface appearance. Animated assets can also carry motion data, although the result depends on the destination application’s support.</p>
<p>For landscape visualization, the <a href="https://shustrik-maps.com/product-category/3d-models/">3D terrain model catalog</a> brings together geographic assets organized by region.</p>

<h3>3D Printing</h3>
<p>Preparing an FBX model for 3D printing requires suitable geometry and a format accepted by the slicer. Exporting to <a href="https://shustrik-maps.com/what-is-an-stl-file-explaining-the-stl-format/">STL</a> may be appropriate, but format conversion alone does not repair an unsuitable mesh.</p>
<p>The printable mesh should define a closed volume with consistent surface orientation and sufficient wall thickness for the intended process. Check the dimensions and inspect the sliced result before printing.</p>
<p>STL preserves a triangulated surface, not the FBX scene’s animation, cameras or material setup. Keep the original FBX and export a separate mesh for fabrication.</p>[/woodmart_text_block][/vc_column][/vc_row]
