South America 3D Models for Renders and Animation

Create geography visuals for websites, presentations, educational illustrations and animation scenes with the 3D Model of South America. Frame the full continent for an overview, or use an oblique camera and directional lighting to highlight the western mountain relief and surrounding lowlands. Its listing includes C4D/OBJ files and a TIF texture.

For a workflow with multiple maps, explore the South America Terrain 3D Model. This C4D variant lists height, normal, satellite, water-mask, pseudo-colour and boundary-mask textures, with OBJ/FBX conversion offered on request. Both models use Mercator projection. Check the chosen product for geometry, texture resolution and file requirements before preparing your scene.
