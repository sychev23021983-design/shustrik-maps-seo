Space Object 3D Models for Lunar Terrain Renders

Explore the Moon at landscape scale with lunar terrain models for digital renders, animation scenes, educational illustrations and website graphics. This collection includes Copernicus Lunar Crater, Theophilus Lunar Crater and a wider Moon Surface model, giving you different crater formations and areas of relief to work with.

The product pages list C4D and STL files, along with TIF textures and normal maps. For digital work, use camera angle and lighting to bring out crater rims, central peaks and the surrounding terrain. Check each listing for its supplied files and texture details, and consider the requirements of your scene before importing the model.

The examples below show a website banner, a terrain flyby still, an editorial background and a shaded relief learning graphic. They offer ideas for presentation and composition. For full-globe scenes, browse the Natural Satellites collection, or explore more assets in the Solar System category.
