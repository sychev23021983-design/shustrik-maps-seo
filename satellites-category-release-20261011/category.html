Moon STL Models for 3D Printing & CNC

Explore the Moon in three dimensions with a digital model of Earth's natural satellite. This category currently features a detailed lunar globe whose cratered surface gives printed sculptures and carved display pieces their distinctive character, from compact astronomy models to statement objects for an interior.

The Moon STL model is offered as a complete globe and separate upper and lower sections at two levels of detail. These versions provide different starting points for full-sphere prints and hemisphere projects, while keeping the lunar surface at the centre of the design.

The examples below show the same model in printed, wood-carved and metallic-finished concepts, with presentation stands and backing added separately.
