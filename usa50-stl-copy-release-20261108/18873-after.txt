<h2>Files and fabrication planning</h2>
<p>The product specification lists a 7.39 GB full archive, 50 meshes with closed bases, triangle geometry, Lambert conformal conic projection and vertical scale factor Z = 3. The archive contains terrain models, not a physical product.</p>
<p>For 3D printing or CNC projects, inspect the files and prepare them for your software, equipment, material and chosen scale. Closed bases alone do not guarantee a successful print, toolpath or fit. Make a small test before producing a complete display.</p>
